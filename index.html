The escape

Your Escape. Your Space. Your unforgettable Naivasha experience.

This beautiful all-en-suite six-bedroom villa is nestled in the scenic surroundings of Naivasha, just off Moi South Lake Road, providing every guest with privacy, comfort, and a touch of luxury.

Designed for relaxation, comfort, memorable getaways and reconnecting, this stunning villa offers the perfect setting for family holidays, group getaways, celebrations, and peaceful weekends away from the city.

Step outside and soak in the beauty of nature. Take a refreshing dip in the swimming pool, gather around the fire pit as the evening sky comes alive, or unwind in one of the villa's multiple sitting areas, each offering opportunities to relax and take in the breathtaking views of Lake Naivasha and Mount Longonot.
